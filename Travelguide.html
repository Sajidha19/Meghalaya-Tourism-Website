Climate Overview




Summer

(June – September)

Summer in Meghalaya is cool and pleasant with comfortable temperatures. The weather is perfect for sightseeing, trekking, and outdoor activities. Green hills, clear rivers, and waterfalls make the landscape beautiful.

Monsoon

(April – May)

Monsoon brings heavy rainfall and turns Meghalaya into a green paradise. Waterfalls like those in Cherrapunji flow at their fullest. Travel can be challenging, but the scenery is breathtaking and magical. Offers stunning landscapes.

Winter

(October – February)

Winter is cool, misty, and one of the best times to visit Meghalaya. Clear skies and pleasant weather make it ideal for sightseeing and photography. The season is perfect for exploring nature, culture, and local festivals.

Autumn

(October – November)

Autumn in Meghalaya is a short and pleasant season after the monsoon. The weather becomes clear, fresh, and ideal for travel and sightseeing. It’s a peaceful time to enjoy green landscapes and cool breezes before winter.






Best Time to Visit


October - June


The best time to visit Meghalaya is from October to April, when the weather is cool, pleasant, and perfect for travel. During these months, the skies are mostly clear, giving beautiful views of the hills, valleys, and landscapes. It is the ideal period for sightseeing, trekking, and exploring natural attractions like waterfalls and caves. Roads are also safer and more comfortable compared to the monsoon season. Waterfalls still look beautiful due to residual greenery, but without heavy rainfall disruptions. This season is perfect for families, honeymoon trips, and solo travelers alike. Among these months, November to February is considered the peak and most enjoyable travel time. Overall, this period offers the best balance of weather, safety, and scenic beauty in Meghalaya.



Smart Travel Tips


Health & Safety


• Carry essential medicines like painkillers, motion sickness tablets, and basic first aid.
• Stay hydrated throughout your journey, especially during treks and outdoor walks.
• Be cautious on slippery paths, especially near waterfalls and during rainy seasons.
• Always follow safety signs and local guidelines while exploring trekking areas.
• Stick to marked trails and avoid risky shortcuts for a safe experience.


Connectivity & Communication


• Mobile networks may be weak or unavailable in remote areas of Meghalaya.
• Download offline maps and travel guides before starting your trip.
• Carry a fully charged phone and a power bank for backup.
• Inform family or friends before visiting isolated or less-connected places.
• Seek help from locals or guides when network is not available.



Weather Preparedness


• Weather in Meghalaya changes quickly, from sunshine to heavy rain.
• Always carry a lightweight raincoat or waterproof jacket.
• Pack warm clothes for cold mornings and high-altitude areas.
• Use waterproof bags to protect gadgets and important documents.
• Check daily weather updates before planning outdoor activities.



Local Travel & Transport


• Local taxis and shared cabs are the best way to travel within Meghalaya.
• Public transport is limited in rural and remote tourist areas.
• Hiring a local driver or guide ensures safer and smoother travel.
• Roads are narrow and hilly, so experienced drivers are recommended.
• Plan extra travel time due to winding hill routes.



Cash & Payments


• Carry enough cash as digital payments may not work everywhere.
• ATMs are mostly available only in major towns like Shillong.
• Small shops and rural vendors prefer cash transactions.
• Keep emergency cash separately for unexpected situations.
• Plan your expenses in advance to avoid inconvenience.




Travel Routes


By Air


• The nearest airport is Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport in Guwahati.
• From the airport, taxis and buses are easily available to Shillong.
• Air travel is the fastest and most convenient option for long-distance visitors.
• The road journey from Guwahati to Meghalaya is scenic and takes about 3 hours.
• It is the most preferred entry route for tourists.



By Train


• Meghalaya does not have its own railway station.
• The nearest major railway station is Guwahati Railway Station in Assam.
• From there, travelers must continue by road to enter Meghalaya.
• Buses, taxis, and shared cabs are available outside the station.
• It is a budget-friendly and commonly used travel option.



By Road


• Meghalaya is well connected to Assam via smooth highways.
• Regular buses and taxis operate between Guwahati and Shillong.
• Road journeys offer stunning views of hills, valleys, and waterfalls.
• Popular destinations like Cherrapunji and Dawki are easily accessible.
• It is the most scenic and adventurous way to travel.
